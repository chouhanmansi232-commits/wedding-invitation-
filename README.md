# Royal Wedding Invitation • Himanshi & Anshul 💍

An elegant, interactive, and mobile-responsive digital wedding invitation card built for **Himanshi & Anshul**, celebrating their wedding on **24th November, 2026** in Jaipur, Rajasthan.

## ✨ Features
- 💌 **Interactive Royal Envelope & Wax Seal**: Animated entrance opening with rose petal confetti burst.
- ⏳ **Live Countdown Timer**: Real-time countdown to 24 November 2026.
- 🎵 **Romantic Background Music**: Ambient wedding flute melody with Web Audio API procedural Tanpura fallback.
- 🌸 **Canvas Petal Physics**: 35+ gently floating rose petals and golden sparkles.
- 📸 **Moments of Love Gallery**: Swiper.js touch carousel and full-screen image lightbox viewer.
- 📅 **Save the Date Integration**: Direct Add to Google Calendar and iCal (.ics) download.
- 💌 **RSVP & Real-time Guestbook**: Guest attendance confirmation and live blessings wall.
- 💬 **WhatsApp Sharing**: One-click sharing with formatted invitation text.
