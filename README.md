# Luxury Editorial Wedding Invitation • Himanshi & Anshul 💍

A premium, elegant, and creative interactive digital wedding invitation with a soft, light, and luxurious aesthetic. Crafted celebrating the wedding of **Himanshi & Anshul** on **24th November, 2026** in Jaipur, Rajasthan.

## ✨ Aesthetic & Key Highlights

- ✉️ **3D Interactive Opening Envelope**:
  - Begins with a closed, physical-feeling luxury textured envelope centered on screen.
  - Realistic gold damask lining and embossed monogram wax seal (`H & A`).
  - Smooth 3D flap opening animation upon interaction.
  - Inner card slides upward with cinematic easing to reveal the couple and invitation blessing before transitioning into the main journey.
  - Replay button (`✉ Invitation Card`) in top navigation allows guests to re-experience the card opening anytime.
- 🕊️ **Warm Light & Luxurious Palette**:
  - Warm ivory, soft cream, champagne, subtle blush, muted beige, and delicate gold tones.
  - Zero dark themes, zero oversaturated colors.
- 📸 **Authentic Couple Photography**:
  - Real, unaltered portraits of the bride, groom, and couple preserved exactly as provided.
  - Royal architectural arch frame in Hero section.
  - Editorial dual-portrait spread for Himanshi (The Bride) & Anshul (The Groom).
- 📅 **Individual Luxury Function Cards**:
  - **Haldi & Phoolon Ki Holi** — Sunday, 22 November 2026 • 10:00 AM • The Palace Central Courtyard
  - **Mehendi & Sangeet Night** — Monday, 23 November 2026 • 6:30 PM • The Grand Durbar Ballroom
  - **The Wedding Ceremony (Varmala & Pheras)** — Tuesday, 24 November 2026 • 6:00 PM • The Royal Heritage Palace, Jaipur
- 🖋️ **Refined Typography**:
  - Cormorant Garamond, Cinzel, Montserrat, and Alex Brush.
- 🌸 **Subtle Ambient Physics**:
  - Ultra-delicate floating jasmine petals and golden stardust canvas.
- 🎵 **Serene Ambient Audio**:
  - Discreet wedding flute melody with Web Audio API procedural synthesis fallback.
- 📱 **Mobile-First & Ultra-Clean**:
  - Zero forms, zero booking or contact clutter, zero intrusive buttons.
  - Fully responsive from mobile screens up to 4K displays.
