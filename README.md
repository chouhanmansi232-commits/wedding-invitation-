# Luxury Editorial Wedding Invitation • Anshul & Himanshi 💍

A premium, elegant, and creative one-page digital wedding invitation inspired by the refined feel of luxury editorial wedding stationery and high-fashion aesthetics. Built celebrating the wedding of **Anshul & Himanshi** on **24th November, 2026** in Jaipur, Rajasthan.

## ✨ Aesthetic & Highlights
- 🕊️ **Warm Light & Editorial Palette**: Warm ivory, soft cream, champagne, muted beige, and subtle gold tones.
- 📸 **Authentic Couple Photography**: Showcasing real portraits in a royal architectural arch and a magazine editorial fine-art spread.
- 🖋️ **Refined Typography**: Cormorant Garamond, Cinzel, Alex Brush, and Montserrat.
- 🌸 **Subtle Ambient Physics**: Ultra-delicate floating jasmine petals and golden stardust canvas.
- 🎵 **Serene Ambient Audio**: Discreet wedding flute melody with Web Audio API procedural synthesis fallback.
- 📍 **Ceremony & Venue Focus**: Clean event details for The Royal Heritage Palace, Jaipur with direct Google Maps & Calendar integration.
- 📱 **Mobile-First & Clean**: Zero bloat, zero forms, completely responsive, and optimized for WhatsApp sharing.
